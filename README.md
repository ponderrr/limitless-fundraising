# Limitless Fundraising

A modern, responsive website for Limitless Fundraising - a Greek life fundraising platform that simplifies fundraising for fraternities and sororities nationwide.

## 🎯 What We Do

Limitless Fundraising handles the strategy, technology, and logistics of fundraising campaigns so Greek life chapters can focus on building community and achieving their goals. We eliminate the stress of spreadsheets and manual tracking with our streamlined platform.

## ✨ Key Features

### For Greek Life Chapters

- **Launch**: We handle setup, strategy, and logistics - you just tell us your goal
- **Share**: Built-in tools for spreading the word via texts, emails, and social media
- **Succeed**: Automated tracking and campaign management from start to finish

### Platform Benefits

- No spreadsheets required
- No manual tracking needed
- Built specifically for Greek life organizations
- Nationwide support for fraternities and sororities
- Results-driven approach

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Styling**: Custom CSS with modern design principles
- **Animations**: CSS animations and transitions
- **Carousel**: Swiper.js for image carousels
- **Email Integration**: EmailJS for contact form functionality
- **Fonts**: Google Fonts (Poppins)
- **Icons**: Font Awesome 6.4.0
